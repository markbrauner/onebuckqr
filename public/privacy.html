Privacy

OneBuckQR is designed to collect as little information as practical. You do not need an account.

Your link

The address you enter is used in your browser to create your QR and is kept temporarily in your browser during checkout. We do not intentionally maintain a customer database of submitted links.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe may collect payment, device, fraud-prevention and contact information according to its own practices. OneBuckQR does not receive or store your full card number.

Operational data

Our hosting and payment providers may create ordinary technical logs for security, reliability and fraud prevention.
