What is a static QR code?

A static QR code stores the destination directly in the code itself. It does not depend on a paid redirect service to keep working. That makes it a simple choice when your destination does not need to change after the QR code is printed.

No QR code subscription

PNG or SVG?

Choose PNG for everyday digital use, websites, documents, and social media. Choose SVG when you need to scale the QR code for print, including posters, signs, packaging, or business cards, without losing sharpness.

When should I use a static QR code?

Static codes work well for links you expect to remain stable: a business website, restaurant menu, donation page, event page, portfolio, social profile, review page, or contact page. If you need to change the destination later without reprinting the code, a dynamic QR service is a better fit.
