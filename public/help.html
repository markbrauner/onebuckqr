Help, without the runaround.

OneBuckQR is intentionally self-service. Most problems should be solvable in under a minute.

Recover unused credits

I bought credits on another device.

Use the recovery page and enter the email from your purchase. We'll send a secure link that restores your remaining credits on this device.

My QR goes to the wrong place.

Static QR destinations cannot be changed after download. Create a new QR with the correct address and test it before printing.

Which download should I use?

PNG is best for ordinary web and social use. SVG is best when a printer, sign shop, designer, or large-format project needs the QR to stay sharp at any size.

Do credits expire?

No. Purchased, unused credits do not expire.

Why isn't there a big support department?

Because that's how a one-dollar product turns into a ten-dollar subscription. OneBuckQR is built to stay automated, simple, and inexpensive.
